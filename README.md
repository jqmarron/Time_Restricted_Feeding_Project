<h1> Prolonged fasting times reap greater geroprotective
effects </h1>
<h2> when combined with caloric restriction in
adult female mice </h2>
TGB. National Institute on Aging. NIH
<h3> Abstract </h3>
Publised on Cell Metabolism
In this study, we reveal greater benefits of a mild reduction of caloric intake over time-restricted feeding in adult female mice, 
highlighting the added contribution of reduced energy intake to further improvement of the effects of daily fasting in terms of fitness, in vivo metabolism, and survival.
